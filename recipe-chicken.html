Lemon Herb Roasted Chicken

This show-stopping roasted chicken is infused with bright lemon, fresh herbs, and garlic. The result is incredibly juicy meat with crispy, golden skin that will impress any dinner guest.

Prep Time 20 min
Cook Time 70 min
Servings 4-6
Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (4-5 pounds)
  • 2 lemons (1 for inside, 1 for juice and zest)
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 tablespoon fresh sage, chopped
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 4 carrots, cut into chunks
  • 4 celery stalks, cut into chunks

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Remove the chicken from the refrigerator 30 minutes before cooking to bring it to room temperature.
  2. Pat the chicken completely dry with paper towels, inside and out. This is crucial for crispy skin.
  3. In a small bowl, mix the softened butter with minced garlic, lemon zest, chopped rosemary, thyme, sage, salt, pepper, and paprika.
  4. Carefully loosen the skin over the chicken breasts and thighs by sliding your fingers underneath. Spread half of the herb butter under the skin.
  5. Rub the remaining herb butter all over the outside of the chicken. Drizzle with olive oil and massage it in.
  6. Cut one lemon in half and stuff it inside the chicken cavity along with a few herb sprigs. Tie the legs together with kitchen twine.
  7. Arrange the onion, carrots, and celery in a roasting pan. Place the chicken on top of the vegetables, breast side up.
  8. Roast for 20 minutes at 425°F, then reduce the temperature to 375°F (190°C) and continue roasting for 50-60 minutes.
  9. The chicken is done when a meat thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the thigh reads 165°F (74°C) and the juices run clear.
  10. Remove from the oven and tent loosely with foil. Let rest for 15 minutes before carving. Squeeze the remaining lemon over the chicken before serving.

Pro Tips

  • Don't skip the resting time—it allows the juices to redistribute throughout the meat.
  • For extra crispy skin, you can air-dry the chicken uncovered in the refrigerator overnight.
  • Save the pan drippings and roasted vegetables to make an incredible gravy.
  • Use a meat thermometer for perfect results every time—guessing can lead to dry chicken.
  • Leftover chicken makes amazing sandwiches, salads, or chicken soup the next day.
