Homemade Tomato Basil Soup

This rich and creamy tomato soup is made with fresh tomatoes, aromatic basil, and a touch of cream. It's the perfect comfort food for a chilly day, especially when paired with a grilled cheese sandwich.

Prep Time 15 min
Cook Time 25 min
Servings 6
Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cans (28 oz each) whole peeled tomatoes
  • 2 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves, packed
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 tablespoon sugar (to balance acidity)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook until soft and translucent, about 5-7 minutes.
  2. Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant, stirring constantly to prevent burning.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes to deepen the flavor.
  4. Add the canned tomatoes with their juices, breaking them up with a wooden spoon. Pour in the broth and add the sugar, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes if using.
  5. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer uncovered for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Remove from heat and stir in the fresh basil leaves. Let them wilt for a minute.
  7. Using an immersion blender, puree the soup until smooth. Alternatively, carefully transfer to a regular blender in batches (be careful with hot liquids!).
  8. Return the soup to the pot if needed, and stir in the heavy cream. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.
  9. Serve hot, garnished with extra basil leaves, a drizzle of cream, or some croutons.

Pro Tips

  • For a lighter version, substitute the heavy cream with half-and-half or coconut milk.
  • Fresh tomatoes work great too! Use about 3 pounds of ripe tomatoes, blanched and peeled.
  • The sugar helps balance the acidity of the tomatoes—adjust to taste.
  • This soup freezes beautifully for up to 3 months. Freeze before adding the cream, then stir it in when reheating.
  • For extra depth, roast the tomatoes in the oven at 400°F for 30 minutes before adding to the pot.
