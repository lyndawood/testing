Our Story

Ember & Oak was born from a simple passion: to bring exceptional coffee to our neighborhood. In 2019, we opened our doors with a single roaster and a dream to create something meaningful.

Every bean we roast tells a story of the farmers who grew it, the land it came from, and the care we put into bringing out its unique character. We believe in direct relationships with our suppliers and sustainable practices that honor both people and planet.

Today, we're proud to be part of this community. Whether you're a coffee enthusiast or just looking for a great cup, we invite you to experience the Ember & Oak difference.
